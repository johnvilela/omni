# Telegram doctor alert: transient outage vs. a guardian timer that stopped clearing alerts (2026-09-02, 17:12–17:25)

Opened with a direct diagnosis ask right after the previous session's doctor work: "Run the command 'omni-dev doctor', you will see various telegram errors. What is causing this? How can we avoid this error?" No plan-first step — went straight to root-causing via `omni-dev doctor`, curl, `journalctl`, and `systemctl` inspection.

Two separate things were going on:

1. **The telegram errors themselves were a transient network outage** (15:22–15:40 that day). Direct `curl -4`/`curl -6` checks against `api.telegram.org` timed out on both IPv4 and IPv6 during that window and succeeded again afterward — the server's `getUpdates` long-poll had simply logged one error per minute until the network came back on its own. Nothing to fix in omni's telegram code; the server recovered by itself.

2. **The real bug was why `omni doctor` still showed red two hours later.** The guardian marked telegram red at 15:35 and then never ran again to clear it. Traced to `omni-dev guardian interval <dur>` writing a timer drop-in `override.conf` that starts with `OnUnitActiveSec=` — an empty assignment resets **all** `On*Sec`/`OnCalendar` clauses in the unit, including the base unit's `OnActiveSec=2min` bootstrap, not just `OnUnitActiveSec`. Left with only `OnUnitActiveSec`, the timer waits on a service activation that never comes: `systemctl --user status` showed `active (elapsed)`, `Trigger: n/a`, and `TimersMonotonic` showed `next_elapse=0` — dead forever, so the stale alert in `guardian.json` never cleared and `omni doctor` kept reporting it long after the network itself had recovered. Full mechanism now recorded in [[gotchas/systemd-timer-dropin-reset]].

Fix: `cli/main.go`'s `runGuardianInterval` (around line 518) now re-adds `OnActiveSec=2min` after the reset, before setting the new `OnUnitActiveSec=<dur>`; version bumped to v0.6.1 per [[rules/bump-version-on-ship]]. Repaired the live `override.conf` by hand, restarted the timer (confirmed rescheduled), and ran the guardian once — it sent a "✅ telegram: recovered" notice to the owner's chat and `omni doctor` returned to "no active guardian alerts"; the 2 still-failing RECENT ERRORS lines were just the outage-window journal entries, expected to age out of the 24h view by the next afternoon.

Side finding, not a bug: the 15:35 guardian journal line printed the **raw bot token** in plaintext — the guardian binary running at that moment predated a journal-token-redaction that has since shipped (the currently installed binary was confirmed via `strings` to mask it as `<token>`). Only in the local journal; flagged to the user with the option to rotate the token via BotFather if that mattered.

Committed as `f732804` — "fix(cli): keep OnActiveSec bootstrap when guardian interval drop-in resets timer clauses" (`cli/main.go`, `version/version.go`).
